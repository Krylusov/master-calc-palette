## Review Focus
- Empty/invalid draft inputs survive interruption without saving invalid receipts (Task 1).
- Unpinned/archived active quantities and exact old durations retain price (Task 2).
- Same-name clients and repeat do not silently overwrite current recipe (Task 3).
- Historical rate snapshots, concurrent edits, revisions and backup retain accounting identity (Task 4).
- Actual-price deductions use actual revenue; offline update retains draft/history (Task 4 and final QA).
